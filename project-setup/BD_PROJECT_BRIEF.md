## The show
- "Boycott Dystopia with Pablos Holman", a Kaleidoscope podcast. Joe is a producer and makes AI sample episodes to show what the show could be. These are not for distribution.
- The idea: the future doesn't have to suck. The world's big problems are unfinished engineering problems, not catastrophes we have to live with.
- Every episode follows the same shape:
  1. **Intro.** Pablos introduces himself and the show, Perplexity's sponsorship, the impossible problem and the guests, then gives a one-line AI-demo note.
  2. **Act one, the problem.** The problem we're told is impossible, and the discovery that might crack it.
  3. **Act two, hot takes.** About 90 seconds per guest, plus Perplexity's take.
  4. **Act three, deep dive.** Pablos picks the most promising idea and pushes hard on the bad news, the money and manufacturing.
  5. **Act four, flip it.** Every technology is a lever, and the question is which way we push it. Each guest flips their company toward saving lives, and Perplexity says where they agree.
  6. **Close.** Perplexity gets the last word, then a break, then Pablos's "future, fixed", then the thanks.
- Perplexity is the presenting sponsor. Pablos asks it short questions live ("in two short sentences") and it answers in its own voice. Open question: its closing verdict is written in Perplexity's style, not taken from a real Perplexity answer.

## Writing and sound rules
- **Delivery:** they are tough businessmen, so use frustration and anger rather than sadness. Swear sparingly (dammit, goddammit, what the hell, bullshit). No f-word.
- **One AI note at the top:** "Quick note: this is an AI demo, so these aren't the real guys or their real voices."
- **Room sounds:**
  - Keep them few and quiet, and tie each one to the speaker: papers before acts, a chair lean-in before heated lines, a breath before long answers.
  - Faint room tone throughout.
  - None under ads and none inside the argument scene.
  - No sips, clothing rustle, table taps, mic bumps or random voices.
- **The CEO line:** Pablos and Perplexity's CEO "discussed our points of view and determined that we actually think about things very similarly and genuinely are seeking to engineer a different world." They did not argue.
